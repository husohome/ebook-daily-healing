# Thought Reframing: Transform Your Inner Dialogue

![CBT Banner: A clear sky emerging from clouds]{.kdp-image-full}

## Understanding Thought Reframing

Thought reframing is a powerful cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) technique that helps you identify, challenge, and modify unhelpful thought patterns into more balanced, realistic perspectives.

### The THINK Model

```markdown
T - Trigger (What happened?)
H - Harmful Thought (What went through your mind?)
I - Investigate Evidence (What are the facts?)
N - New Perspective (What's another way to see this?)
K - Knowledge Gained (What did you learn?)
```

### 4. Perspective Shift

#### For Analytical Types
Complete this logical analysis:
```markdown
If someone else was in this situation...
What would I tell them?
What evidence would I point to?
```

#### For Emotional Types
Explore the emotional landscape:
```markdown
How would I feel about this in a week?
What would my wisest self say?
What would self-compassion look like here?
```

#### For Action-Oriented Types
Focus on solutions:
```markdown
What's one small step I can take?
What's in my control right now?
What action would serve me best?
```

#### For Reflective Types
Consider the bigger picture:
```markdown
What might I learn from this?
How does this fit into my growth?
What wisdom can I gain?
```
