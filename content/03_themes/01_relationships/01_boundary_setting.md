# Setting Healthy Boundaries: Nurturing Relationships Through Self-Respect

![Boundaries Banner: Peaceful garden with fence]{.kdp-image-full}

## Understanding Boundaries

Healthy boundaries are the invisible lines that define where you end and others begin. They protect your energy, values, and well-being while allowing for meaningful connections.

### Types of Boundaries

1. **Physical** - Touch, personal space, time
2. **Emotional** - Feelings, energy, support
3. **Mental** - Thoughts, values, opinions
4. **Digital** - Online presence, communication
5. **Material** - Possessions, money, resources
